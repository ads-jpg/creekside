# Tiami Recurring Meeting Agenda: Budget & Performance (30 min)

1. **Budget check (5 min).** Agreed daily/monthly cap per channel, spend to date, projected month-end, and any channel pacing >10% over.
2. **Performance review (10 min).** Purchases, cost per purchase, and ROAS by channel, compared with last period.
3. **Changes since last meeting (5 min).** Every pause, launch, or budget change, and confirm each one was approved in writing.
4. **Decisions (5 min).** Channel on/off, new caps, and priorities. **Read back every budget number out loud before the meeting ends.**
5. **Wrap-up (5 min).** Creekside posts a written recap in Slack within 24 hours (caps, decisions, owners). The client replies "confirmed."

Use `meeting-budget-checklist.csv` as the live tracker during each call.
