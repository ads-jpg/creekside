Definitions and what's included
Form fill
The Meta pixel's custom "form_start" event, which is what the campaign optimizes for.
Lead
The Meta pixel's standard Lead event.
Appointment booked
The Meta pixel's Schedule event. Where Meta reports only a cost per Schedule, the count is spend divided by that cost, rounded.
Cost per …
Spend divided by that count. Blank when the count is zero.
Week
Monday to Sunday in the ad account's time zone. The prior week is the seven days before.
Attribution
The ad account's default attribution setting in Meta Ads Manager.
