## Before you record: Meta's rules in plain language

Meta reviews the ad *and* the landing page. These scripts are written to pass. If you ad-lib, keep to these:

- **Talk about yourself ("I used to…") or about women in general ("So many women I work with…").** Never "you" tied to a health trait. "Are you exhausted?", "your thyroid," and "Tired of snapping at your kids?" can all get the ad rejected.
- **Don't open by making her feel bad about herself.** Open with your story, a scene, or a possibility.
- **No promised results or timeframes.** Use "there may be a way…"
- **Never say:** cure, treat, fix, heal, reverse, prevent, adrenal fatigue, or any named condition. No price. No "spots filling" or "last chance."
- **Composite stories** are introduced as "a pattern I see often," never as one woman's results.

**Filming tips:** natural light facing a window, phone at eye level, speak to one woman. The first line has to land in under 3 seconds, so start talking right away. Add captions (most people watch muted).

## Compliance check (all ads)

- First person or "women in general" only; no "you" tied to energy, mood, or health
- No negative self-image hooks; every hook is a story, a scene, or a reframe
- No promised outcomes or timeframes; possibility language only
- No cure / treat / fix / heal / reverse / prevent; no named conditions
- No price, no "spots filling," no countdowns, no exclamation points
- No comparisons with other doctors
- Composite story introduced as "a pattern I see often"
- Ask the ads specialist to target by **interests and location only** (Oregon, since appointments are telehealth under her Oregon license), never by health conditions
