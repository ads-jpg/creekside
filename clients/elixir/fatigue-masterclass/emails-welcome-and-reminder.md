## Zoom recommendation

A regular Zoom meeting works for this. Recommended setup:

1. **Turn off Zoom's registration.** She's already registering on the landing page. Zoom registration adds a second form and sends its own confirmation email that competes with this one. Use the meeting's standard join link (with the passcode embedded) as `{{join_link}}`.
2. **Reconsider "everyone has to be let in by the host."** Admitting people one by one while presenting live is hard, and late joiners get stuck. Zoom requires a passcode *or* a waiting room; keep the embedded passcode and turn the waiting room off, or keep it and have a co-host admit people.
3. **Keep "mute participants on entry."** Also turn off participant screen sharing and set chat to "Host and co-hosts" or "Everyone" depending on how she wants Q&A to run.
4. **Recording:** use cloud recording (Pro plan) so the replay link is ready for the follow-up email.
5. **Capacity:** check the plan's participant limit (Pro is 100 by default) against expected registrations.
6. **Booking link:** have the call-booking link ready to paste into chat during the offer.
