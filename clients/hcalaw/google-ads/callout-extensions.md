## Verify before launch

- **`Free Case Consultation`** - A Justia-syndicated directory lists 'Free Consultation' beside the firm's number; confirm on site.
- **`Direct Attorney Access`** - Credible for a 3-person firm, but only if callers reach an attorney, not an intake service.
- **`Walk-Ins Welcome`** - From the Yelp listing. Confirm it still holds.
- **`Pender & Brunswick Co.`** - Only if the firm appears in those courts and geo-targeting reaches them.
- **`Top 100 Trial Lawyers`** - National Trial Lawyers designation on Allard's profile - confirm the year is current.

## Do NOT run these

- **`No Fee Unless We Win`** - Contingency language - personal injury only, meaningless in criminal defense.
- **`Best Defense Attorney`** - Unverifiable superlative. NC Rule 7.1; Google flags these too.
- **`Board Certified in DWI`** - NC Rule 7.4 requires NC State Bar Board of Legal Specialization certification.
- **`Charges Dropped`** - Past-results claim. NC Rule 7.1 requires a disclaimer that won't fit in 25 chars.
- **`Call (910) 763-0156`** - Google disallows phone numbers and CTAs in callouts. Use a call asset.
- **`Available 24/7`** - Posted hours are Mon-Thu 9-5, Fri 9-3.

## How to load them

Put the ten Core callouts at the **account level** so every campaign inherits a floor.
Attach each practice group at the **ad group level**, matched to keyword intent - a search for
"DWI lawyer Wilmington" should see DWI callouts, not drug-charge ones. Google serves up to ten
and picks per auction, so 6-10 relevant assets per ad group beats loading all twenty.
Callouts are not clickable: keep them as proof, not instruction. No CTAs, no offers, no
punctuation tricks. Title Case throughout.

Run `python3 verify_callout_lengths.py` to re-check every string against the 25-char limit.
