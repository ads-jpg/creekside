# Criminal Defense RSA Copy - policy-safe, reusable

Three complete responsive search ads for criminal defense, written to clear Google policy
review. Nothing names a controlled substance, a weapon, a sex offense, or an outcome. No
field asserts a credential, year count, or result, so nothing needs substantiating before
launch - the one thing to confirm is that consultations really are free.

**Limits:** headline 30 chars (max 15, min 3) | description 90 chars (max 4, min 2) | path 15 chars

## Pull these from the other asset sets

The same filters hit callouts and snippets. Already removed from this repo:

- `Sex Crime Defense` and `Drug Trafficking Defense` - from the callouts
- `Sex Crimes` - from the Types structured snippet

The firm can still take those cases and describe them on the website. The classifier reads
ad assets, and a practice area named in a 25-character asset has no room for the context
that makes it obviously a defense service.

## Why this vertical gets flagged

Criminal defense advertising is lawful and Google permits it. What trips review is automated
classification - a defense attorney's vocabulary overlaps almost exactly with the vocabulary
of the things Google restricts.

| Policy | Triggers | Why it fires on defense copy |
|---|---|---|
| Dangerous products or services | Named substances, trafficking, weapons, firearms | The classifier sees the substance, not the defense context. |
| Sexual content | Sex offenses, assault terminology | Highest sensitivity. Almost nothing survives review; appeals rarely land. |
| Misrepresentation | Outcome promises, guarantees, superlatives | Unverifiable claims. Also breaches most state bar advertising rules. |
| Enabling dishonest behavior | Copy reading as help evading consequences | "Beat your charge", "avoid jail" cross from defending to escaping. |
| Restricted: bail bonds | Bail, bonds, bail bondsman | Restricted outright in the US. Not a review question - it will not run. |

## Substitutions

| Don't write | Write instead | Note |
|---|---|---|
| ~~Drug Trafficking Defense~~ | `Felony Defense` | Or "Serious Charges". Keep detail on the landing page. |
| ~~Sex Crime Attorney~~ | `Serious Felony Defense` | Do not name the offense in any ad asset. |
| ~~Gun / Weapons Charge~~ | `Felony Defense` | Firearms terms trip the dangerous-products filter. |
| ~~Get Your Charges Dropped~~ | `Understand Your Options` | Outcome promise. Also a bar-rules problem. |
| ~~Beat Your DWI~~ | `DWI Defense Representation` | Reads as evading a lawful process. |
| ~~Best Criminal Lawyer~~ | `Experienced Defense Attorney` | Unverifiable superlative. |
| ~~Avoid Jail Time~~ | `Know What You're Facing` | Same category as the above. |
| ~~Bail Bonds Available~~ | - | No substitute. Restricted category, full stop. |

## Pinning

Pin almost nothing - every pin removes combinations Google could test, and a fully pinned RSA
is an expanded text ad with worse reach. The one defensible pin is a single service headline in
position 1 so the ad always names the practice area. If a disclaimer or bar-required line must
appear, pin it to Description 2; pinning two or more assets to the same position lets Google
rotate among just those.

## When an ad is disapproved

Ad-level disapproval is routine and does not endanger the account. What escalates to suspension
is a pattern - the same flagged copy pushed through repeatedly, which reads as circumvention.

1. **Read which policy fired.** The disapproval names it; guessing wastes a resubmission.
2. **Edit the asset, don't resubmit it.** In an RSA you can delete the one offending headline and keep the ad.
3. **Appeal once, with context.** Worth it for a false positive on a general defense term; not for sexual content.
4. **Isolate anything sensitive** in its own ad group so a disapproval stays contained.
5. **Check the landing page.** Review covers the destination, not just the ad.

## Never put these in an ad

- `Guaranteed results`, `We win`, `100% success` - misrepresentation, and a bar violation everywhere
- `Board certified` unless actually certified by the state bar's specialization board
- Named substances - cocaine, meth, heroin, opioids
- Any sex offense term
- `Bail`, `Bond`, `Bail bondsman` - restricted, and attracts the wrong searcher
- `ALL CAPS`, `!!!`, `Call NOW` - editorial policy, rejected on formatting alone

Run `python3 verify_rsa_copy.py` to check every field against its limit (exits non-zero on any violation).
