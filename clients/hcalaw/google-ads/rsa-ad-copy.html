What changed

Three verticals down to two. Traffic & License is held rather than deleted — its keywords, callouts and sitelinks stay in the repo and switch back on whenever you want them. Cheap clicks and low case value made it the obvious cut, and removing it stops it competing for budget with the two campaigns that carry the revenue.

Two competitor claims we are not copying

“NC State Bar certified lawyer.” Under NC Rule 7.4 that is a specialist claim, and it requires certification from the NC State Bar Board of Legal Specialization. Their attorney presumably holds it. Nothing in this firm’s record shows the same, so it stays out of the copy — if the firm does hold it, tell me and it goes into both ads immediately, because it is the one place they currently outrank us on credentials.

“NHTSA sobriety test knowledge.” A specific field-sobriety credential. Same rule: only if the attorney actually holds it.

Confirm the review count before this runs

200+ positive reviews appears in five assets across the set, so it needs to be right. The directory record I could reach showed a 4.9 rating on a handful of Martindale reviews, which is a different number from a different place — presumably the 200+ is the Google Business Profile. Confirm the source and that the count is current, since a review claim in a legal ad has to hold up.

Why this vertical gets flagged

Criminal defense advertising is lawful and Google permits it. What trips review is automated classification: the systems match on subject matter, and a defense attorney's vocabulary overlaps almost exactly with the vocabulary of the things Google restricts.

PolicyWhat triggers itWhy it fires on defense copy
Dangerous products or services Named substances, trafficking, weapons, firearms The classifier sees the substance or weapon, not the defense context. "Drug trafficking defense" reads to the system much like an ad for the thing itself.
Sexual content Sex offenses, assault terminology Highest-sensitivity category. Almost nothing survives review here regardless of framing, and appeals rarely land.
Misrepresentation Outcome promises, guarantees, superlatives "Charges dropped", "we win", "best" — unverifiable claims. This one also breaches most state bar advertising rules.
Enabling dishonest behavior Copy reading as help evading consequences "Beat your charge", "avoid jail" cross from defending someone into helping them escape a lawful process.
Restricted: bail bonds Bail, bonds, bail bondsman Restricted outright in the US. Not a review question — it simply will not run.

Pinning

Pin almost nothing. Every pin removes combinations Google could otherwise test, and a fully pinned RSA is just an expanded text ad with worse reach. The one defensible pin is a single service headline in position 1 so the ad always names the practice area — leave everything else free to rotate.

If a specific claim ever needs to appear

Pin it rather than hoping it shows. A firm that must display a disclaimer, a licensing statement, or a bar-required line should pin it to Description 2, which serves more reliably than an unpinned asset. Note that pinning two or more assets to the same position lets Google rotate among just those — that's the way to keep several required lines in play without collapsing the ad down to one variant.

When an ad is disapproved

An ad-level disapproval is routine and does not endanger the account. What escalates to a suspension is a pattern — the same flagged copy pushed through repeatedly, which reads to Google as circumvention rather than error.

  • 1
    Read which policy firedThe disapproval names it. "Dangerous products" and "Misrepresentation" call for different fixes, and guessing wastes a resubmission.
  • 2
    Edit the asset, don't resubmit itIn an RSA you can usually delete the one offending headline and keep the ad. Resubmitting identical copy is the behavior that turns an ad problem into an account problem.
  • 3
    Appeal once, with contextWorth it for a clear false positive on a general defense term. Not worth it for anything in the sexual content category — that appeal almost never succeeds.
  • 4
    Isolate anything sensitiveKeep borderline practice areas in their own ad group. A disapproval there stays contained instead of taking a working ad group down with it.
  • 5
    Check the landing page tooReview covers the destination, not just the ad. A page heavy on offense terminology can flag ads whose own copy is clean.
