Three Campaigns, Not Seven

How to structure the Google Ads account for hcalaw.net. The instinct with a firm this broad is one campaign per practice area — that's the expensive mistake here. A three-person firm in a market Wilmington's size cannot spread a budget across seven campaigns and still gather enough signal in any of them to optimize.

The rule that decides everything

Split into a separate campaign only when you need separate budget, geography, or schedule. Everything else — relevance, ad copy, landing pages, keyword themes — is an ad group's job. Every campaign you add divides the same money into a smaller pot that takes longer to learn.

Campaign level Controls the money Budget, bidding strategy, geo targeting, ad schedule, networks, language. Add a campaign when one of these must differ.
Ad group level Controls the match Keyword theme, ad copy, landing page, structured snippet override. Add an ad group when the searcher's problem differs.

Traffic tickets are the clearest case. They convert at a few dollars a click while DWI runs $20–45 in this market, so in a shared budget the cheap clicks win the auction all day and DWI quietly starves. That difference in click economics — not the fact that they're different practice areas — is what earns traffic its own campaign.

How many campaigns your budget supports

Campaign count is a function of budget, not of how many things the firm does. A campaign needs roughly 15–30 conversions a month before automated bidding has anything to learn from; below that you are paying Google to guess.

Monthly budgetCampaignsWhat runsReasoning
Under $1,500 1 DWI / DUI only At $20–45 a click this buys 35–70 clicks. Spread across two campaigns, neither gets enough data to optimize. Concentrate on the highest-value case type.
$1,500 – $3,500 2 DWI · Criminal Defense The realistic starting point for a firm this size. Two budgets you can actually read.
$3,500 – $7,000 3 + Traffic & License Traffic earns its place once there's enough budget that its cheap clicks won't cannibalize DWI.
$7,000+ 4 + Brand protection Also unlock the Federal ad group, and test Search Partners as a separate experiment.

Wilmington is a small market, so these CPCs sit well below the $75–200 seen for DWI in large metros — but criminal defense is still one of the most expensive verticals in paid search. Confirm real numbers with Keyword Planner against the actual geo before committing a budget.

Settings that spend money on default

These are the defaults Google's campaign creation flow sets for you. Each one costs real money in a local legal account, and all of them are set at campaign level.

SettingDefaultSet toWhy it matters here
Location options Presence or interest Presence The single biggest leak. On the default, someone in Ohio reading about NC DWI law triggers the ad. You want people physically in the service area.
Search Partners On Off Lower-intent inventory you can't see or segment well. Test as its own experiment later, not on day one.
Display expansion On Off Nobody browsing a recipe site hires a DWI lawyer. This is pure leakage on a Search campaign.
Auto-apply recommendations Several on All off Account-level, easy to miss. Left on, Google will add broad match keywords and raise budgets without asking.
Broad match Suggested Phrase + exact Broad match needs conversion data and smart bidding to steer it. With neither, it just spends.
Networks Search + Display Search only Same reasoning. Add other channels deliberately, never by leaving a checkbox alone.

Bidding, in order

Do not start on Target CPA. It needs conversion history the account doesn't have yet, and it will either refuse to spend or spend badly.

PhaseStrategyMove on when
Weeks 1–6Maximize Clicks with a max CPC cap around $35–40The cap is the guardrail. Without it this strategy will find you the most expensive clicks available.
Phase 2Maximize Conversions~15 conversions/month in the campaign, tracking verified accurate.
Phase 3Target CPA~30 conversions/month. Set the target from the CPA you actually achieved, not from a wish.

Each campaign graduates on its own schedule. DWI will get there first; Traffic may never need to leave Maximize Clicks.

Conversion tracking comes first

None of the above works without this, and it's the step most often half-done. Criminal defense leads call — they do not fill out forms at 2am after an arrest. If you only track form fills you will conclude the campaign failed while it was working.

  • 1
    Calls from adsConversions from the call asset. Count only calls over 60 seconds — that filters wrong numbers and hang-ups.
  • 2
    Calls from the websiteNeeds a Google forwarding number on the site so a click-then-call is attributed back to the campaign. Without it, most of the value is invisible.
  • 3
    Form submissionsSecondary volume for this vertical, but still worth a distinct action so you can compare lead quality by source.
  • 4
    One primary action per campaignCalls primary, forms secondary. Multiple primary actions double-count and mislead the bidding algorithm.
  • CHECK
    Intake is the real conversionA tracked call is not a signed case. Ask the firm to log which calls became consultations — that's the number that tells you whether any of this is working.

Negative keywords, day one

Build these as a shared negative list applied to all campaigns before launch, not after you've paid for the lesson. The two big categories are people who won't pay and people looking for a different kind of lawyer.

Won't convert

free · pro bono · public defender · legal aid · cheap · court appointed · how to · what is · definition · penalty · sentence · jobs · salary · internship

Wrong practice area

divorce · custody · child support · personal injury · car accident · injury · bankruptcy · immigration · workers comp · estate · will · probate · disability

Wrong intent

bail bonds · bond · inmate · jail records · mugshot · arrest records · court records · warrant search · reviews · complaints · disbarred

Then read the Search Terms report weekly for the first month and monthly after. That report, not the keyword list, is where the waste actually shows up.

Geo and schedule

Target New Hanover County as the core, with a radius of roughly 25–30 miles around downtown Wilmington. Add Pender and Brunswick counties only if the firm actually appears in those courts — the same caveat as the geo callouts.

The scheduling problem worth raising with the client

Posted hours are Mon–Thu 9–5 and Fri 9–3. But people are arrested on Friday and Saturday nights, and they search for a lawyer the next morning. If you only run ads during office hours you miss the highest-intent window in this entire vertical; if you run them around the clock into an unanswered phone, you pay for clicks that ring out. Neither is acceptable. The fix is an answering service or after-hours intake, and it is a business decision that will move cost-per-case more than any setting on this page. Until that's resolved, run the full week with bids reduced overnight and treat the gap as a known cost.

Also worth running: Local Services Ads

Google Screened sits above the Search results, and bills per lead

Google's Local Services Ads carry Criminal Lawyer, DUI Lawyer and Traffic Lawyer as categories, all three of which fit this firm. They appear above the paid Search block, charge per lead rather than per click, and carry the Google Screened badge — which requires a background and license check on the firm and its principals, plus a Google Business Profile rating of 3.0 or better. For a small firm competing against larger advertisers on cost-per-click, this often returns better than Search does. It's a separate product from everything above, so it doesn't change the campaign structure — run it alongside, and start the screening process early because verification takes weeks.

Don't run these

  • NO
    Performance MaxIt needs conversion volume this account won't have for months, gives almost no visibility into where spend went, and will absorb brand searches and claim credit for them. Revisit only once Search is profitable and tracking is trusted.
  • NO
    Display or VideoHiring a criminal defense lawyer is an urgent, high-intent search. There is no awareness funnel to build here at this budget.
  • NO
    A campaign per practice areaSeven campaigns at this budget means seven campaigns with too little data to optimize. Use ad groups.
  • NO
    Broad match at launchWithout conversion data and smart bidding to steer it, broad match is just a wider net over the same budget.
  • NO
    Bail bonds keywordsGoogle restricts bail bond services advertising outright. The firm doesn't offer it, and the terms attract the wrong searcher regardless.
