How to read this

  • Numbers come straight from Meta and refresh every 15 minutes. Meta can revise the last day or two as late conversions come in.
  • A qualified call is a call from the ad that lasts at least 20 seconds.
  • CPMs on lead campaigns run high because housing and credit ads use Meta's special ad category, which limits targeting.
