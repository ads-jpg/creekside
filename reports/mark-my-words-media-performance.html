What we see

  • Lead campaign's first run produced nothing. CS | Leads spent $684 from Sep 15–22 with zero form fills, then went dark until Sep 29. Since the relaunch it has 3 fills on $491 ($164 each).
  • Form Start is the volume engine. 5.14% CTR and $0.66 CPC are the best in the account, and it is spending about $135/day. Two fills in five days puts it at $337 per fill so far, which is still early.
  • Traffic is cheap but not built for leads. It drives 60% of impressions for 15% of spend. Its two fills on Oct 4 are welcome but shouldn't be counted on.
