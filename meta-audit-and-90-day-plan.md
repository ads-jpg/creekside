# Fusion Dental
## Meta Audit and 90-Day Plan

## AUDIT

### Overview
We've completed a full review of your Meta ad account to evaluate performance, tracking limitations, and opportunities for optimization as we take over management.

Because your account falls under Meta's health & wellness category, there are inherent restrictions that impact tracking and optimization. Despite this, your campaigns are already producing solid results, and with a few strategic adjustments, we believe we can improve efficiency, lead quality, and overall performance.

### Key Finding: Tracking Limitations
Inside Meta Events Manager, we identified that:
- Custom events (consultations, no-shows, CRM actions) are being blocked by Meta
- The pixel is primarily tracking:
  - Page views only

What this means:
- We cannot rely on standard conversion tracking
- Optimization must rely on:
  - platform signals (lead forms, engagement)
  - campaign structure
  - creative performance

This is common in restricted categories, but it requires a more strategic media buying approach.

### Campaign Structure Issues
**Mixed Objectives Within Campaigns**

We observed campaigns using:
- Instant Forms
- Messenger
- Website Leads

within the same campaign structure.

Why this is a problem:
- Meta cannot properly optimize when objectives are inconsistent.

Recommendation:
- Each campaign should have one clear objective
- Separate campaigns for:
  - Lead Forms
  - Messaging
  - Traffic (if tested)

### Targeting Observations
You are currently segmenting by:
- Spanish-speaking audiences
- English-speaking audiences

Opportunity:
- Additional language segments (ex: Russian) could be tested
- However, this should be done:
  - in separate ad sets
  - with intentional creative and messaging

### Placements Optimization
Currently, Advantage+ placements are heavily used.

Recommendation:
- Refine placements to avoid wasted spend:
  - Turn OFF:
    - Audience Network
    - Low-intent placements
- Goal:
  - Focus budget on placements with higher conversion likelihood

### Budget & Campaign Efficiency
We noticed:
- Some campaigns have significantly better performance
- Higher budget campaigns tend to perform better due to:
  - more data
  - better optimization

Recommendation:
- Instead of spreading budget across many campaigns:
  - Focus on 3–4 top-performing offers/messages
- This allows:
  - stronger optimization
  - lower cost per lead
  - clearer performance insights

### Creative Strategy Issues
Current approach:
- Multiple creatives per ad set (4–5 images/videos)

Problem:
- Budget is diluted across underperforming creatives
- Top performers are not fully leveraged

Example:
- One ad produced ~80 results
- Others significantly underperformed

Recommendation:
- Shift to a focused creative strategy:
  - Run 1–2 primary creatives
  - Scale what is working
  - Introduce new creatives only when:
    - performance drops
    - frequency increases (≈3+)

### Lead Quality Challenges
Lead forms are currently the primary conversion method.

Issue:
- Meta lead forms often produce:
  - lower intent leads
  - incomplete or inaccurate information

Recommendation:
- Add pre-qualifying questions, such as:
  - Why are you interested in treatment?
  - Current dental situation
  - Timeline for treatment

This helps filter out low-quality leads before submission.

### New Funnel Opportunities
To improve both volume and quality, we recommend expanding beyond lead forms:

**1. Engagement Campaigns (High Opportunity)**

Use:
- Video content
- Before/after visuals
- Strong pain-point messaging

Drive users to:
- Engage
- Message directly

Then implement:
- automated responses
- guided conversation flows
- appointment links

Benefit:
- Faster response time
- Higher intent interactions
- Lower cost per initial contact

**2. Top-of-Funnel Campaigns**

Introduce:
- awareness videos
- educational content

Goal:
- build trust
- warm audiences

**3. Traffic Campaign Testing**

Even with limited tracking:
- Sending traffic to a high-converting landing page can still work

Especially if:
- messaging is strong
- page is optimized

### Creative Direction Recommendations
Some messaging is already performing well, especially:
- Pain-point driven messaging
- "Tired of dentures" angles
- Financial incentive offers

Moving forward:
- Focus on:
  - Emotional triggers (comfort, confidence, frustration)
  - Transformation messaging
  - Credibility and expertise

### Next Steps
As we take over management, our focus will be:
- Clean up campaign structure
- Consolidate and optimize top-performing offers
- Improve creative efficiency
- Increase lead quality through form optimization
- Introduce engagement + messaging funnels
- Expand top-of-funnel strategy

### Final Thoughts
Despite platform restrictions, your account already has a strong foundation.

With a more focused structure and strategic testing approach, we are confident we can:
- Lower cost per lead
- Improve lead quality
- Increase booked consultations

## 90-DAY PLAN

### Strategic Objective
Over the next 90 days, our focus will be to:
- Improve lead quality while maintaining volume
- Reduce cost per patient acquisition
- Build a scalable Meta system for Fusion Dental implants

### Phase 1: Foundation + Implant Optimization (Days 1–30)
**Primary Focus: Stabilize + Improve Implant Campaigns**

**1. Campaign Restructure & Efficiency Improvements**

We will immediately optimize the current implant campaigns:
- Standardize campaign objectives (Lead Forms vs Messaging)
- Consolidate top-performing campaigns
- Reduce wasted spend

Key actions:
- Eliminate mixed objectives
- Reduce creative overload
- Focus on top-performing ads
- Refine placements

**2. Creative Strategy Reset (Implants)**

Shift messaging toward:
- Pain + urgency (functional need)
- Transformation (confidence, lifestyle improvement)
- Authority (highly experienced doctor positioning)

This is critical given the high-value nature of implant patients.

**3. Lead Quality Improvements**
- Add pre-qualification questions to forms
- Introduce messaging campaigns for higher-intent conversations
- Improve ad messaging to better filter leads

**4. Messaging Funnel Implementation**

We will introduce messaging campaigns to:
- Drive faster engagement
- Reduce friction
- Move prospects toward consultation faster

Outcome of Phase 1:
- Lower cost per lead
- Improved lead quality
- Clear understanding of top-performing messaging

### Phase 2: Creative Iteration + Scaling (Days 31–90)
**Primary Focus: Scale What's Working, Refine What Isn't**

- Increase budget on top-performing implant campaigns
- Continuously refresh creative to prevent fatigue (watch frequency ≈3+)
- Expand winning angles into additional formats (video, carousel, UGC)
- Layer in engagement and top-of-funnel campaigns to feed the lead pipeline
- Refine placements and audiences based on Phase 1 learnings

### Retargeting (Fusion General Dentistry & Fusion Cosmetics)
Although those brands are managed as separate businesses, we will run Meta retargeting campaigns toward their audiences (site visitors, engagers, and existing patient lists where permitted). This re-engages warm traffic without mixing ad accounts or muddying campaign signals.

### Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
We will measure success based on:
- Cost per qualified lead
- Cost per consultation
- Cost per patient acquisition
- Lead quality indicators
- Volume by campaign and offer

### Strategic Advantage
This approach allows us to:
- Focus Meta investment on the implant offer
- Build a long-term growth engine through disciplined testing and creative iteration
- Extend reach through retargeting to aligned audiences across the other Fusion brands
