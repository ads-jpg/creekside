Before You Book

Veneers questions, answered

How much do veneers cost at Vida Dentistry?

Veneers typically range from $2,000 to $3,000 per tooth. Your exact investment depends on how many teeth are being treated and what your plan includes. We review the full breakdown with you at your consultation, and customized financing options are available.

Do you offer payment plans or financing for veneers?

Yes. We work with Cherry, CareCredit, Alphaeon Credit and Proceed Finance. Cherry offers 0% APR plans of up to 24 months for qualified applicants, and Proceed Finance offers terms of up to 12 years. Checking what you qualify for uses a soft credit check only, so it will not affect your credit score. All financing is subject to credit approval, and terms vary by provider and applicant.

Am I a candidate for minimal-prep or no-prep veneers?

We offer both minimal-prep and no-prep veneers, but whether you are a candidate depends on your current tooth position, bite, and overall smile goals. We always take a biomimetic, ultra-conservative approach to preserve as much natural tooth structure as possible, and in many cases we use Invisalign beforehand to help align teeth in a way that reduces or completely eliminates the need for extensive tooth prep.

Can I see what my smile will look like before committing?

Yes. Dr. Vida uses hand-sculpted, freehand removable material to temporarily enhance the shape, size, and color of your teeth right in the chair — no drilling and no commitment required. You get to see and feel a sample smile before you decide to move forward.

How long do porcelain veneers last?

Our handcrafted porcelain veneers are engineered for durability and beauty. With proper care, they can last 15 to 20 years or longer.

How long does the veneers process take?

The timeline depends on the treatments included in your plan. Veneers themselves generally take a few weeks from design to final placement. If alignment or other groundwork is part of your plan, we map the full schedule out for you at your consultation.

Why is biomimetic dentistry better for long-term oral health?

By avoiding aggressive enamel removal and restoring teeth in a way that mimics natural function, biomimetic treatments reduce stress on your bite and protect the surrounding tissues.

Is there a fee for the veneers consultation?

The consultation is $199, and it is fully credited toward your treatment if you move forward with your smile transformation.
