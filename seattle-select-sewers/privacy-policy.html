Privacy Policy — seattleselectsewers.com

Last updated: September 12, 2026

Our Privacy Policy

Your privacy is important to us. We never sell or give your information to third parties without your consent. Our policy is to disclose our information gathering and dissemination practices so that you may make informed decisions with respect to the disclosure of your personal information.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Information sharing to subcontractors in support services, such as customer service, is permitted. All other use case categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties.

What Information We Collect

In using the seattleselectsewers.com web site (the "Site"), you may be asked to provide us with various information. For example, when you request service, schedule an appointment, or submit an inquiry at our Site, you will be asked to provide your first and last name, phone number, e-mail address, and the ZIP code or service address where the work is needed, along with a description of the problem you are having. In addition, when you browse our Site or use our services, the choices you make may provide information concerning your interests or site visitors' areas of focus.

How We Use Information

We use the information you provide to process your request, to schedule and dispatch a technician, to prepare estimates, and to provide notification about appointments or inquiries regarding our sewer and drain services — including drain and rooter clearing, leak detection, hydro jetting, sewer camera inspection, and trenchless sewer line repair — and for other purposes specific to activity at our Site or use of the services provided.

In addition, we may use e-mail addresses, phone numbers, or other contact information for marketing purposes. For example, we may send notification about new services, seasonal maintenance reminders, or promotions that we or a partner are offering. We do not sell your information to third parties.

How to Opt Out or Update

If you would prefer not to receive e-mails or other information from us, simply e-mail us at contact@seattleselectsewers.com and put the word REMOVE in the body of the e-mail. To stop receiving text messages, reply STOP to any message you receive from us. We may still contact you to inform you of a problem with the service or a change that would affect the way you use it. If you receive an e-mail newsletter, you may opt out by clicking "unsubscribe" in the newsletter.

In addition, if you request it, we will use commercially reasonable efforts to remove your name and personal information from our database. Please understand, however, that it may be impossible to remove this information completely due to backups and records of deletions. If you wish to remove your name from our database, send an e-mail to contact@seattleselectsewers.com and type "delete records" in the subject line.

Cookies

Your Internet web browser has a feature called "cookies." Cookies allow a site to recognize you while you are visiting. We and our advertising partners use cookies to remember your preferences and to measure how our Site and our advertising perform. You can disable cookies in your browser settings; our Site will still work, though some features and measurement may be limited.

Privacy Exceptions

There are two important exceptions to our privacy policy. First, we reserve the right to release or use personal information in connection with legal proceedings or in response to a subpoena or court order. Second, our policy applies only to information you provide to us. You may provide information to third parties if, for example, you click on third-party advertising or a link to a third-party site. We cannot be responsible for the conduct of these third parties. They may have policies concerning privacy and the use of cookies that differ substantially from ours. You may receive unsolicited messages from these third parties, or from others, as a result of that conduct.

Third-Party Cookies

In the course of serving advertisements to this Site, our third-party advertisers may place or recognize a unique cookie on your browser.

Your Consent

By using our Site, you consent to our collection and use of information in accordance with this privacy policy. We may in the future decide to change this privacy policy. If we decide to change it, we will post the change on this page so that you are always aware of what information we collect, how we use it, and the circumstances under which we disclose it.
