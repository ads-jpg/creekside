Before sending

  • Strap offer terms. The copy says "buy any 2 cameras, get a beaded strap free ($55 value)". Confirm this matches how the offer is set up in Shopify.
  • Black Friday prices. These are the site's prices with BC25 applied as of Nov 19, 2026: Digicam $127.50, Bestie Bundle $240, Creator Pack $160, Travel Kit $176.25, Case $35.25.
  • Review quotes. The quotes in emails 1, 2 and 5 are placeholders. Replace them with real customer reviews.
  • Footer tags. The footer uses Klaviyo's unsubscribe, preferences and address tags. Swap them if you send from another platform.
  • Tracking. Every link carries utm_source=email, utm_medium=email and a utm_campaign named after the email.
